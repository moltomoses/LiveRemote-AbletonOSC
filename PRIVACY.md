# LiveRemote Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

LiveRemote is a controller for Ableton Live and connected MIDI equipment. The app does not require an account, include advertising or analytics, or send your usage or music data to a developer-operated server. This policy describes the app's own handling of data; websites you choose to open have their own policies.

## What stays in the app

LiveRemote saves settings and performance data in its local app storage. These can include your Mac hostname or IP address, MIDI mappings and routing, theme preferences, selected tracks and devices, favorite parameters, X/Y assignments, presets, snapshots, chords, and patterns. Track, device, and parameter names or values may be included in saved performance data. The app does not provide an account or an app-operated cloud sync service.

## Connections you use

LiveRemote exchanges control messages and state with the Mac you configure using AbletonOSC over your network. This includes information such as track and clip names, device parameters, mixer values, and transport timing. MIDI notes and control messages travel through your connected network MIDI session; connected USB or Bluetooth controllers can send MIDI into the app. These connections are part of operating your music setup. The app does not upload these messages to a developer-operated service.

## Demo mode

Demo mode uses sample tracks and controls without connecting to your Mac or sending hardware MIDI. Demo performance changes are separate from your saved setup and reset when you begin a new demo session. The demo does not produce audio.

## Permissions and backups

Local Network access allows LiveRemote to discover and communicate with your music setup. Bluetooth access is used when you choose Bluetooth MIDI equipment. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. Depending on your Apple device and backup settings, local app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. Those backups are managed by Apple and your chosen backup service, not by LiveRemote.

## Support and external websites

Opening a policy, support, setup, or companion-credit link takes you to GitHub, a separate service. LiveRemote does not automatically attach diagnostics or app data to a report. Information you choose to submit is handled by GitHub, and issues in the public support repository are publicly visible. Avoid posting private project details, credentials, or other personal information. Privacy questions can also be raised through the support page below.

## Your choices

You can edit settings and delete saved presets, snapshots, and library patterns in the app. Deleting LiveRemote from your device removes its local app data; offloading the app keeps that data. Existing backups and any GitHub content you submitted are managed separately through their respective services.

## Policy changes

This policy reflects the features in this version of LiveRemote. If the app's data practices change, the bundled policy and its effective date will be updated.

## Contact

For support and privacy questions, use the [LiveRemote GitHub issue page](https://github.com/moltomoses/LiveRemote-AbletonOSC/issues). Issues posted there are public.
