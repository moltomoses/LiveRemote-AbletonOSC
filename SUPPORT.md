# LiveRemote Support

LiveRemote is an independent iPhone and iPad controller for Ableton Live. It communicates with your configured Mac through the LiveRemote AbletonOSC companion and a network MIDI session.

## Explore without a Mac

Open Settings and choose Try Interactive Demo, or choose the demo in the first-run setup assistant. You can explore sample tracks, clips, mixer controls, device parameters, and performance tools. The demo is silent and sends no MIDI or commands to Live. Demo changes are separate from your saved setup and reset when you begin a new demo session.

Choose Exit Interactive Demo in Settings when you want to connect to your music setup.

## Set up your Mac

Follow the [LiveRemote companion installation guide](https://github.com/moltomoses/LiveRemote-AbletonOSC). In the app, open Settings and run the setup assistant. The assistant checks AbletonOSC and network MIDI separately.

If Live controls respond but pads or keys do not, check the network MIDI session in Audio MIDI Setup on your Mac. In Ableton Live, enable Track for the network MIDI input and arm the track you want to play.

## Report a problem

Use the [LiveRemote GitHub issue page](https://github.com/moltomoses/LiveRemote-AbletonOSC/issues). Include your LiveRemote version, iOS version, Ableton Live version, what you tried, and what happened. GitHub may require an account to post.

The issue page is public. Review information and screenshots before submitting them, and avoid posting credentials, private project details, or other personal information. LiveRemote does not automatically attach diagnostics to reports.

## Privacy

The [LiveRemote Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) describes local app storage, connections to your music setup, device backups, and support reports. The same policy is available offline in the app under Privacy & Support.
